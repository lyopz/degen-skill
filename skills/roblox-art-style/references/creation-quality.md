# Creation quality levels: how good everything we make must be (lyopz, 2026-10-25)

<!-- from: lyopz (2026-10-25): "Creation Skill Upgrade" sheet (creation-quality/creation-levels-sheet.jpg): before/after for bodies, eggs, crates, buildings, horns, halos, accessories, skill effects + 7 levels. -->

This is the quality bar for everything the Lead and agents create. **Default target: level 5–7** for every asset type. Never ship level 1–3 work as final; those levels are only for blockouts.

| Lvl | Name | What it has |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | simple shapes, basic materials, no effects, minimal detail (blockout only) |
| 2 | Improved | more detail, better materials, simple glow, small decals |
| 3 | Advanced | unique shapes, glow effects, small particles |
| 4 | Premium | complex design, animated parts, multiple materials, particle effects |
| 5 | Elite | high-detail model, animated effects, advanced VFX, unique theme |
| 6 | Mythic | intricate design, multiple animations, strong VFX, rare theme |
| 7 | Ultimate | maximum detail, dynamic animations, layered VFX, signature theme |

Match the level to the item's rarity: Common/Uncommon items at 5, Rare/Epic at 5–6, Legendary and up at 6–7. Hero pieces (hub buildings, main eggs, crates) at 6–7.

## Before → after, per asset type (what "after" means)
- **Pet base body:** a plain cube becomes a dark armoured shell with glowing seams, crystals breaking through, floating shards, and its own idle animation.
- **Eggs:** a plain egg becomes a gold-framed shell with crystal panels, a glowing core, an orbit ring and floating shards, all themed to its island.
- **Crates:** a plain box becomes a themed armoured crate with glowing panels, a crystal top, a halo ring, floating pieces and an opening animation.
- **Buildings:** a grey block becomes a large detailed structure with towers, lights, banners, waterfalls/greenery and a VFX beam or ring. It's a landmark you can read from far away.
- **Horns / halos / wings / accessories:** plain shapes become layered, faceted pieces with flames, crystals, orbiting rings and floating parts, each with an animation.
- **Skills / item effects:** a flat ring becomes a layered effect (portal core, swirling ribbons, shards, sparks).

## How to get there
Same steps as `pet-upgrades.md` (glow → auras/particles → depth/layers → full version), with the low-poly budgets and subtle-glow rules still applying. Glow lives on edges, cores and effects. Every asset gets a theme and at least one animation.
