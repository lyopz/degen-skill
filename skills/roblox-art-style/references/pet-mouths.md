For new mouth designs, keep the same feel: a **wide hand-drawn grin**. Rarer pets get a more dramatic version of the same grin. References he sent: `pet-mouths/liked-grin.jpg` (the feel) and `pet-mouths/mouth-ideas.jpg` (21 mouth styles + 3-side wrap). Attach them in ChatGPT, or use as `-Ref`, when the mouth comes out wrong.

> **Game items come from `~/RobloxGames/<game>/pet-catalog.md` only** (`pet-parts.md`). The styles listed here are ideas for upgrades or new designs when lyopz asks, not items in a game.

## The look (all rarities)
- **Wraps around 3 sides (front, left, right)**, following the cube's rounded edges, corners curving up toward the cheeks on the side faces. Sits low, under the eyes. Same body size and shape for every mouth.
- **Chunky, uneven white teeth**, each a slightly different size and tilt, like hand-cut paper. Upper and lower rows meet (closed bite) on lower tiers.
- **Black ink drawing style**: thin black gaps between teeth, **crosshatch / pen-scratch shading** at the corners and along the gumline, rough sketchy edges. It looks drawn on with a pen, on top of the glossy 3D body.
- **No outline in another colour, no lips, no tongue, no pink.** Mouth is only white teeth + black ink + the body colour.
- Flat, matte teeth. The body around it stays glossy and premium (`chatgpt-prompts.md` → "Premium polished pets").

Clean variant (from his accessory sheets, 2026-10-24): bold wide white grin with black gaps and no crosshatch. Same grin family; use it with big accessories (`pet-accessories.md`).

## Slop (never)
- A plain rounded open mouth: smooth capsule/oval hole, dark inside, with a smooth coloured outline (orange/gold ring). Clean vector shapes with no ink.
- Cartoon smiles: simple curved line, "U" smile, tongue, pink inside, cute small mouth.
- Perfect identical teeth in a neat row (looks like a zipper or a keyboard).

## Rarity tiers (same grin, more drama)
Every tier wraps the 3 sides; higher tiers reach further back and up the sides.

| Tier | Reach | Teeth | Ink + extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | just round the front corners | 8–10 chunky flat teeth, closed bite | light crosshatch only at the two corners |
| Uncommon | a quarter onto each side | 10–12, one tooth a bit crooked | crosshatch at corners + thin gumline scratches |
| Rare | half of each side, corners curl up higher | 12–14, one chipped tooth, one small gap | heavier crosshatch, sketch lines trailing from corners |
| Epic | most of each side | mix of square teeth and 2–4 short pointed fangs | dense ink shading, rough scribble edge |
| Legendary | full sides, corners up near the top edge | jagged teeth, slightly parted bite showing a black inked inside, 2 long fangs | ink cracks spreading from the corners; thin glow line in the pet's element colour along the ink (subtle, ≤ 0.4) |
| Mythic / Huge / Secret | full "Cheshire" grin, almost to the back | most jagged, uneven rows, longest fangs | ink drips/scratches leaking from the corners, glowing element-colour edge on the teeth; animated (slow grin widen or ink shimmer, handoff to vfx-artist) |

Keep it readable from far away: the grin is the face's main shape, eyes stay small and sharp. When a game's bible names its own rarities, map them onto these rows from the bottom up.

## Mouth styles (from his sheet, sorted by rarity)
Each style is a variation of the same wide 3-side grin. Keep the grin feel in every one: wide crescent, chunky uneven teeth, and a **thin black ink edge / crosshatch at the corners** even on glowing styles. Glowing ones stay subtle (glow ≤ 0.6) and are elemental, so they get animation (taste rule).

| Tier | Styles | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Classic Grin, Skew Grin, Asym Jaw | white chunky teeth; skew = one side higher; asym = one side open wider |
| Uncommon | Sharp Fangs, Bone Jaw, Split Jaw, Slit Mouth | ivory fangs; bone-coloured long teeth; thin needle teeth split in two rows; slit = thin glowing crescent line, no teeth |
| Rare | Spike Teeth, Double Mouth, Neon Stripe, Robotic Jaw | blue-glow spikes; two rows of jaws stacked; glowing stripe band with dark ink edges; metal jaw plates with glowing teeth |
| Epic | Energy Maw, Toxic Ooze, Crystal Teeth, Matrix Teeth | glowing zigzag fangs (any element colour); green teeth with slime drips; faceted ice crystals; green code-like bar teeth |
| Legendary | Demon Fangs, Void Teeth, Cyber Fangs, Cracked Core | red-orange fangs + matching glowing eyes; purple void teeth + slanted eyes; cyan cyber plates and fangs; glowing cracks running from the mouth with drips |
| Mythic / Secret | Hollow Void, Full Mask | dark hollow inside ringed by glowing purple fangs; armored face mask with the mouth carved in, glowing eyes |

A pet's tier picks the row; pick the style that fits the pet's element (fire → Demon Fangs, ice → Crystal Teeth, toxic → Toxic Ooze, digital → Matrix/Cyber, void → Void/Hollow). New styles can be added; keep each one a grin.

**As unlockable accessories ("Mouths crate"):** the mouth is its own part that swaps onto the same base body. Build each style once as a separate mouth piece (below), give it the crate rarity from this table, and store it per game (local). Only build a crate in a game when lyopz asks for it.

## Prompt words (part 4 "Face" of the 8-part prompt)
Base line, every pet:
"Mouth: a wide hand-drawn grin wrapping around three sides of the cube (front, left and right faces), following the rounded edges, corners curving up on the sides, chunky uneven white teeth of slightly different sizes, thin black ink gaps between the teeth, black pen crosshatch shading at the corners and along the gumline, rough sketchy ink edges, like an ink illustration drawn onto the glossy body; matte white teeth; no lips, no tongue, no coloured outline, not a round open cartoon mouth."

Then add the style line (the style's look from the table, e.g. "Crystal Teeth style: faceted icy blue crystal teeth, subtle glow") and the tier line:
- Common: "grin about half the face wide, closed bite, light crosshatch only at the corners."
- Uncommon: "grin a bit wider than half the face, closed bite, one crooked tooth."
- Rare: "grin about 70% of the face wide with corners curling up high, one chipped tooth and a small gap, heavier crosshatch, sketch lines trailing from the corners."
- Epic: "grin almost edge to edge, wrapping slightly around the front bevel, a few short pointed fangs among the square teeth, dense ink shading."
- Legendary: "grin wrapping onto both side faces, jagged teeth slightly parted showing a black inked inside, two long fangs, ink cracks spreading from the corners with a thin <element colour> glow line."
- Mythic/Huge/Secret: "huge Cheshire grin wrapping most of the way around the body, very jagged uneven teeth, longest fangs, ink drips and scratches leaking from the corners, thin glowing <element colour> edge on the teeth, still clean and readable."

## On the 3D model (Customuse → Unity → Studio)
Image-to-3D bakes the mouth into the texture, but a 1024 texture often blurs the ink. Check it in the Unity shots:
1. **Ink crisp and the wrap reads on all 3 sides in the shots?** Keep it. In `PaintRegions`, put the teeth rule **before** silver so the teeth don't turn metal: `{"label":"teeth","hueMin":0,"hueMax":360,"satMin":0,"satMax":0.12,"valMin":0.85,"metal":0,"smooth":0.3,"glow":0}` (see `roblox-3d-pipeline/references/unity-polish.md`).
2. **Blurry, melted or lumpy teeth, or a crate accessory?** Make the mouth separate: ChatGPT picture of **only the grin unrolled flat (front in the middle, left and right sides at the ends), on a plain #00FF00 green background** (white teeth won't cut out from white), same prompt words + tier line. Key out the green to a transparent PNG (`python skills/roblox-art-style/scripts/chroma-key.py in.png out.png`), save it in `~/RobloxGames/<game>/models/<pet>/mouth.png` (local only).
3. **Studio:** upload `mouth.png` with `roblox-upload.ps1` (decal) and put it on a **thin mouth band MeshPart** that hugs the front and both sides of the body (follows the rounded corners, ~0.02 studs off the surface), welded to the body, named `Mouth`. One band mesh per base body; each style is just a different texture on it (fangs/crystals/jaw plates that stick out = small extra meshes on the band). This is also the crate accessory: swap the `Mouth` part. Quick fallback on a flat-sided cube: three `Decal`s (Front, Left, Right) cut from the unrolled picture.
4. Keep one mouth per rarity per game in the game's bible ("Mouths" section, local) so every pet of that tier uses the same grin.
