# lyopz's design taste

Read before every design (Customuse, ChatGPT, Blender). Updated after every rating by `customuse-design` step 7.
Format: `- <rule> (<date>, <design>)`. Newest wins when two lines disagree.

## Rules (hard, always / never)
- Elemental design = animated (rule): anything with fire, galaxy, lightning, matrix code or another element in its design gets subtle animation (flicker, swirl, crackle, scroll, orbit) for cool visuals - never a static element. (2026-10-24, lyopz)
- Build it exactly how lyopz describes it; his words beat any preset. (2026-10-22, roblox-art-style)
- Smooth, light, clean shapes; never a lumpy decimated mesh. (2026-10-23, models)
- One asset per picture, white background. (2026-10-23, pictures)
- Glossy bold game UI (2x liked): white cards with thick colored borders, chunky outlined titles, rarity/variant-colored tiles, big green primary + red delete buttons. Never thin flat "web" UI. (2026-10-24, play session x2)

- Glows are subtle: far less bright than default — small emissive accents, low bloom, never blown-out neon sheets. (2026-10-23, game review)
- Accessories/parts are real shaped models that follow their reference picture (horns, wings, feet with their curves and details), never "a cube with more cubes"; refine in Blender until it matches the picture. (2026-10-23, game review)
- "Slop" = generic, plain, primitive-block look (plain eggs, box buildings). Every hero asset (eggs, buildings, pets) needs a reference picture and a designed silhouette. (2026-10-23, game review)

## Likes
- Cartoon stylized low-poly with super high quality finish (like his approved concept pictures): chunky readable shapes, clean bevels, glossy, rich color. (2026-10-23, game review)
- Movement that feels fun: double jump, trampolines/launch pads, cloud hopping instead of obby stairs; worlds that feel immersive with several goals at once. (2026-10-23, game review)
- Galaxy skies: cartoon galaxy backdrop, darker at the start, hue shifting as you climb higher. (2026-10-23, game review)
- Pets polished and premium: sleek silhouette, small sharp gem eyes, matte body + glossy armor + gold trim + glowing crystal accents, rim light, rich deep colors. Use the "Premium polished pets" block in roblox-art-style/references/chatgpt-prompts.md. (2026-10-23, pets)
- Big elemental "demon/dragon" pets: horns, bat or feather wings, crystal shards, glowing eyes, neon edge glow, one strong element color each (fire orange, ice blue, void purple, toxic green); large and shown off around the player. (2026-10-23, play session)
- Glossy bold game UI: white cards with thick colored border, chunky outlined titles, rarity-colored item cards with variant ribbons, big green buttons. (2026-10-23, play session)
- Zones with one dominant color + neon accents (e.g. dark rock with glowing purple cracks, crystal flowers, floating rocks). (2026-10-23, play session)
- Glowing crack lines on dark rock as accents (portal floors, egg pedestals, lava eggs/pets); the cracks lead the eye to the landmark. Thin lines, not glowing sheets. (2026-10-24, play session, liked 2x)
- Themed portals/landmarks: each arch dressed in its island's theme (pumpkins + graves, dark crystal rock, mushrooms) with a swirl in that island's color and a bold label. (2026-10-24, play session)
- Eggs as designed creatures: themed shell (lava rock with glowing cracks, horns, small bat wings), not a plain colored egg. (2026-10-24, play session)
- Magma/elemental cube pets: dark cube body with glowing cracks + angry glowing eyes; one bright hero pet (phoenix) among dark ones. (2026-10-24, play session)
- Collectible-card pet details: tilted card, variant-colored glowing border + corner ribbon, stars, tag pills, stat rows, level bar. (2026-10-24, play session)
- Tiered upgrades shown as a row of colored cards, each with its own themed icon (shoe, spring, tornado, wings...). (2026-10-24, play session)

- Chunky cartoon low-poly models in the big-pet-sim style: thick readable silhouettes, smooth soft shading, flat saturated colors, dark elemental bodies with thin glowing cracks; he asked for whole games built in this style "in detail". (2026-10-24, play session)

## Dislikes
- Cartoonish pets: round blobs, huge googly eyes, big smiles, pastel candy colors, flat plastic look. (2026-10-23, pets)
