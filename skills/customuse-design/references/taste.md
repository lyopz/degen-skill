# lyopz's design taste

Read before every design (Customuse, ChatGPT, Blender). Updated after every rating by `customuse-design` step 7.
Format: `- <rule> (<date>, <design>)`. Newest wins when two lines disagree.

## Rules (hard, always / never)
- Build it exactly how lyopz describes it; his words beat any preset. (2026-10-22, roblox-art-style)
- Smooth, light, clean shapes; never a lumpy decimated mesh. (2026-10-23, models)
- One asset per picture, white background. (2026-10-23, pictures)

## Likes
- Pets polished and premium: sleek silhouette, small sharp gem eyes, matte body + glossy armor + gold trim + glowing crystal accents, rim light, rich deep colors. Use the "Premium polished pets" block in roblox-art-style/references/chatgpt-prompts.md. (2026-10-23, pets)

## Dislikes
- Cartoonish pets: round blobs, huge googly eyes, big smiles, pastel candy colors, flat plastic look. (2026-10-23, pets)
